Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Fleeterbase is a Broadway Pixels service for managing rental vehicles, reservations, maintenance, revenue, and connected fleet tools. This policy explains the information processed when you use fleeterbase.com.

Information we process

  • Account information such as your name, login email, securely hashed password, business profile, and preferences.
  • Workspace records you enter or import, including vehicles, VINs, license plates, reservations, guest names, trip dates, earnings, maintenance records, and manual location check-ins.
  • Connected-service data you authorize. Bouncie may provide vehicle identifiers, locations, speed, trip, and diagnostic events. Gmail read-only access is used to find Turo messages and extract reviewable trip fields. Complete Gmail message bodies are not retained in your Fleeterbase workspace.
  • Billing identifiers and subscription status supplied by Stripe. Fleeterbase does not receive or store complete payment-card numbers.
  • Security and operational information such as session records, login-attempt counters, webhook receipts, timestamps, and service logs.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, sync workspace records, provide fleet and map features, process selected imports, operate subscriptions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers and connections

Fleeterbase uses Cloudflare for application hosting and database services. Optional connections involve Google, Bouncie, and Stripe under their own terms and privacy policies. Information is shared with a provider only as needed for the feature you request or as required by law.

Retention and security

Cloud workspace data remains while your account is active. OAuth credentials are encrypted separately from workspace records, session tokens are stored as hashes, and passwords use a salted adaptive hash. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so keep your credentials private and report suspected account misuse.

Your choices

You can export or reset workspace records in Settings, disconnect Google or Bouncie, manage Stripe billing through the Customer Portal, change your password, or permanently delete the account in Settings → Security. Account deletion requires your current password and active Pro subscriptions must be canceled first. Provider and transaction records controlled by Google, Bouncie, Stripe, or applicable law may follow those providers’ retention rules.

Children and changes

Fleeterbase is a business operations service and is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as features and providers change and will revise the effective date when we do.

Contact

For privacy requests, email support@broadwaypixels.com from your Fleeterbase account email. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or payment-card details.
