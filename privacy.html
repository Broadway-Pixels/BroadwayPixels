Tanktopia

Privacy policy

What the current local build does, what may change with production providers, and how to request help with your data.

A clear view of the current build

Last updated 2026-08-03. This policy describes Tanktopia as it exists today and will be revised before material production data services are enabled.

01

Current local and offline behavior

Tanktopia is an in-development mobile game. The current core gameplay is designed to run locally and offline, without a sign-in requirement. Game progress and settings are saved on the device. The current build uses local or mock service behavior while production credentials and provider integrations remain unfinished.

02

What is enabled now

Rewarded-ad placements, purchase flows, receipt handling, analytics forwarding, cloud saves, push notifications, and related backend behavior are local, mocked, buffered, optional, or disabled unless their production SDK and configuration are installed. A local support diagnostic can be copied or exported by the player for troubleshooting.

03

Conditional production providers

If a future build enables them, providers such as AppLovin MAX, a store and receipt service, Firebase or another analytics service, cloud-save services, or a push provider may process the information needed for those features. That can include purchase receipts, device or user identifiers, gameplay or product events, crash data, performance data, or push tokens. Provider use will depend on the shipped build and its configuration.

04

No blanket tracking claim

The current local or mock build does not forward analytics or ad events to production providers unless the corresponding SDK and configuration are enabled. This page does not promise that a future provider-backed build will be identical. Any enabled tracking or other material data use should be disclosed in the app and in an updated policy before that change.

05

Your export and delete route

In Tanktopia, open Settings > Privacy & Data to use the available local export and delete tools. If a production build has provider-backed data that cannot be removed from the device, email support@broadwaypixels.com to request access or deletion. Include the request, platform, and any relevant player or purchase reference, but do not send passwords or payment details.

06

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or support, contact support@broadwaypixels.com or use the Broadway Pixels support page. Production providers, retention, and data practices may change as Tanktopia moves beyond its current local and offline stage. This page does not represent store approval or store availability.
