Steady

Privacy policy

How the Steady fitness and weight-management app handles your account, wellness data, coaching, purchases, and privacy choices.

Your data, explained clearly

Last updated 2026-08-27. Steady is a wellness product from Broadway Pixels, not a medical service.

01

Information you choose to provide

Steady can store your name, age, sex, height, weight goals, activity level, diet and workout preferences, food and water logs, weight entries, workouts, steps, measurements, reminder choices, and optional GLP-1 preferences. Medication details and symptom tracking are optional. Account users also provide an email address for authentication. Please do not enter information you do not want Steady to process.

02

Local use and account sync

You can use core tracking features in local mode, where app data is stored on your device. If you create or sign in to a Steady account, Supabase provides authentication, database, and storage services so supported data can sync across devices. Private cloud records are protected with user-specific database permissions. Internet transmission and cloud storage can never be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

03

AI Coach

For signed-in users, Steady may send your question, a compact structured summary of relevant tracked data, and recent conversation context through a protected server function to OpenAI. This lets the Coach tailor wellness guidance without exposing an API key in the app. Coach messages are stored with your Steady account to preserve conversation history. Separate usage records keep operational details such as model, tier, response time, safety category, and token counts without copying your prompt, reply, or health snapshot into those usage records.

04

Health integrations

Apple Health is optional and is not required for manual tracking. When HealthKit support is enabled, Steady will request permission for each supported data type and use approved information to provide app features such as activity and progress tracking. Health data will not be used for advertising. You can change Apple Health permissions in iOS settings. Features that are not enabled in the current build do not collect data.

05

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may process product, purchase, subscription, receipt, entitlement, store account, and device information needed to offer, validate, restore, and manage Steady Pro. Broadway Pixels does not receive your full payment-card number. Store and RevenueCat identifiers may be linked to your Steady account so paid access can sync across supported devices.

06

Advertising and consent

The free version may display ads supplied by Google Mobile Ads. Where required, Steady asks for advertising consent before requesting ads and provides privacy choices through Settings. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized in the current implementation. Google and the applicable platform may process device, consent, diagnostic, and ad interaction information under their own policies. Pro and trial access remove Steady ad placements.

07

How information is used

We use information to operate accounts and sync, calculate goals and progress, display nutrition and activity history, provide Coach responses, manage subscriptions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, protect the service, comply with law, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use health information to determine eligibility for insurance, employment, credit, housing, or similar decisions.

08

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you reset the app, remove its data, or uninstall it. Cloud data is retained while your account is active and as reasonably needed for security, legal, support, and transaction records. Signed-in users can open Settings > Data & Privacy > Delete account and data to permanently delete their Steady account, synced app records, Coach history, private progress-photo files, and local app data. You can also request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing support@broadwaypixels.com from the account email. We may need to verify your identity. Deleting a Steady account does not cancel a store subscription, and purchase records controlled by Apple or Google must be managed through those platforms.

09

Children and safety

Steady is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher minimum age required where they live, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Steady provides general wellness guidance and does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or change medication doses. Contact an appropriate healthcare professional for medical concerns and emergency services for urgent symptoms.

10

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Steady and its providers change. Material changes will be reflected by the date above and, when appropriate, an in-app notice. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@broadwaypixels.com or use the Broadway Pixels support page.
