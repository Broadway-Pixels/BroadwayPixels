Frequently Asked Questions

Is any of your music made with AI?

No. Every Broadway Pixels release is written, arranged, produced, and finished by me. I do not use AI to create my music. AI is used in some software projects and video workflows, but never to make the songs.

How do you use AI?

I use AI as a creative and development tool in some apps and video projects, including features, workflows, and visuals.
